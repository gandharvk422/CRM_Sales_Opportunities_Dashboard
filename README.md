# CRM Sales Opportunities Dashboard: Go-to-Market & Commercial Excellence Analytics

**Overview**
This repository features a business-focused CRM Sales Opportunities Dashboard specifically designed to analyze B2B sales pipeline performance, revenue opportunities, and sales team effectiveness. Developed using Microsoft Excel, this project demonstrates the quantitative analysis and problem-solving skills necessary to translate raw data into clear, actionable insights for commercial strategy. 

The workflow simulates the rigorous data interpretation required in Commercial Excellence and Go-to-Market (GTM) delivery teams, focusing on identifying opportunities for improvement across key commercial levers.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives
Sales organizations generate massive volumes of CRM data, but raw data alone fails to provide visibility into team productivity, account-level revenue, or pipeline health. The objective of this project is to provide a structured reporting solution that helps business stakeholders:
* Monitor B2B sales pipeline performance and track revenue outcomes.
* Identify high-value opportunities and support data-driven commercial decisions.
* Evaluate sales capacity, route to market efficiency, and overall salesforce effectiveness.

## 💡 Key Commercial Insights Generated
This project transforms operational data into strategic commercial recommendations, aligning with core GTM levers:
* **Salesforce Effectiveness:** Tracks which sales representatives drive the strongest performance and identifies bottlenecks reducing conversion efficiency.
* **Account Planning Frameworks:** Identifies which accounts contribute the most to revenue generation to help prioritize high-performing accounts.
* **Market Opportunity & Pipeline Health:** Evaluates which pipeline stages contain the highest revenue opportunities and monitors win/loss conversion rates.
* **Product Performance:** Evaluates product-level sales impact to support pricing and marketing strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technical Skills
This project was built to showcase structured problem solving and proficiency in the tools central to commercial analytics workflows:
* **Microsoft Excel & Google Sheets:** Advanced data cleaning, structuring, Pivot Tables, and KPI calculations to build an interactive business reporting dashboard. This directly demonstrates the hands-on Excel proficiency required for live practical assessments.
* **Data Visualization:** Executive-level reporting layout with dynamic filtering and segmentation.
* **Future Scalability:** Structured to easily migrate to advanced visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI in the future.
